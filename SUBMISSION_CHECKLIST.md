   > *"We used Avaloka to investigate 'The Monsoon Recharge Gap' across Gujarat using 33 years (1991–2023) of official CGWB/NWIC records (52,919 observations across 34 districts). We discovered that across 410 station-years, aquifers experienced zero or negative recharge despite monsoon rains, with Mehsana reaching a 12.5% recharge deficit rate. Furthermore, we unmasked Simpson's Paradox: Saurashtra's hard-rock basalt wells show rapid seasonal recharge (4.65m) but lose 34% by January due to low rock storativity (Sy 1–3%), whereas North Gujarat suffers from chronic deep alluvial depression (13.5 mbgl) and a 2.6x higher recharge failure rate.
